Robotic Arm for Feeding People with Disabilities
Overview

Many people with limited hand or arm mobility depend on others to help them eat. This project is a low-cost robotic feeding arm designed to let users eat independently, which improves dignity, comfort, and independence.

Objective

Design and build a mechatronic robotic arm that can pick up food from a plate or bowl and bring it safely to the user’s mouth.
